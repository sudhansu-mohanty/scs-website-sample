Workshops, tutoring nights, and exam jams that help you get through the hard courses alongside people who have already been there.

Events

Explore what SCS has to offer

Events

Competitions

Test your skills, build under pressure, and go head-to-head with the best. SCS organises and supports competitions that span hackathons, programming contests, and industry challenges — all open to Concordia students.

Annual

HackConcordia

Concordia's flagship 24-hour hackathon. Build a project from scratch, pitch to sponsors, and compete for prizes — teams of up to four, all disciplines welcome.

Annual

ICPC Regional

The ACM International Collegiate Programming Contest North America qualifier. Three-person teams race the clock solving algorithmic problems — a true test of CS fundamentals.

Sponsored

SCS Case Competition

An industry-sponsored challenge where teams analyse a real-world tech problem and present a solution to a panel of judges. Sharpens communication as much as technical thinking.

Open Night

Code Golf Night

Solve a set of programming puzzles in the fewest characters possible. A light-hearted evening that rewards creativity and tricks with the language — all skill levels welcome.
