## About

SCS Concordia is the **Software Engineering and Computer Science Society** at Concordia University. We are Concordia's premier tech community, dedicated to supporting students in their journey through software engineering and computer science — through networking, technical development, and exciting competitions.

---

## Recurring Events

### AlgoTime
- **When:** Every Monday, 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- **What:** Weekly LeetCode practice sessions open to all experience levels. Come work through coding challenges, improve your problem-solving skills, and connect with fellow developers.

### Tech Hour
- **When:** Every Wednesday, 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM
- **What:** Hands-on sessions where you build a software project within 2 hours and try out the newest APIs and AI tools.

## Networking & Social Programming

SCS hosts a variety of events throughout the year to help members grow academically and professionally:

- **Academic Events** — Workshops, speaker panels, and study sessions
- **Wine & Cheese** — Annual networking event featuring **12+ companies** and **25+ professionals and recruiters**
- **Social Events** — Movie nights, gaming sessions, and casual hangouts

## Competitions

### Hello World Hackathon
- **When:** Mid-September
- **Focus:** Beginner-friendly creative coding event emphasizing teamwork and project-building

### CS Games
- **Tryouts:** January / February
- **Competition:** March
- **Focus:** Inter-university competitive programming event

### CyberSci
- **When:** Mid-November
- **Focus:** Canada's national cybersecurity competition (regional qualifiers + nationals)

### NorthSec
- **When:** May
- **Focus:** One of North America's largest Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions

### ICPC
- **Tryouts:** Fall
- **Focus:** Global algorithmic programming competition requiring team-based problem solving
